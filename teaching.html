1. Modern Homotopy Theory

I have delivered a few lectures on an online series on Modern Homotopy Theory. Here I talk about abstract and concrete homotopy theories, in the form of abstract categorical language. This way one can prove that most of our known old mathematical theories are actually Homotopy theories inherently. To find more about it visit the webpage below.

2. ISLA 2022

Indian School for Logic and its Application, ISLA 2022 was an international summer school on Logic and Dualities, organized by IIT Kanpur in May 2022. I have delivered 3 introductory lectures on basic category theory with an introduction to the notion of categorical dualities. This was a wonderful experience to present before such a diverse audience, starting from Philosophy, logic, mathematics, and Topology to Computer science students from all over the globe.
